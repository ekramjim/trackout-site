Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

TrackOut doesn't collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer can't see anything you do in the app.

Apple Health

With your permission, TrackOut reads your workouts and related data from Apple Health: heart rate, active and resting energy, distance and workout effort. It uses this only to show your workouts and to compare them over time.

  • TrackOut never writes to Health.
  • Health data stays on your device. It isn't sent to the developer, isn't stored in iCloud by TrackOut, and is never shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • You can change what TrackOut can read at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › TrackOut.

What you enter

The exercises, sets, weights, plans and notes you enter are stored on your device and synced through your own private iCloud account, so they appear on your other devices. Only you can access this data; the developer can't. You can delete it by deleting the app, and remove it from iCloud in Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

On-device intelligence

Workout summaries and Ask TrackOut use Apple Intelligence on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer or to a server. Summaries are kept on your device only.

Notifications, Siri and Shortcuts

Notifications are created on your device. Siri and Shortcuts actions run on your device.

Children

TrackOut isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
