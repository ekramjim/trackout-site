Common questions

My workouts don't show up.
Allow Workouts in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › TrackOut. TrackOut shows workouts once they're saved to Health.

My session didn't finish when I ended the workout on Apple Watch.
TrackOut attaches the session once the workout reaches Health on your iPhone, usually within a minute. Workouts shorter than about a minute, or discarded on the Watch, aren't saved to Health; finish those sessions from the ••• menu in the session.

How do I get a notification when a workout starts?
In TrackOut, open Settings › Workout Notifications and follow the steps to add a Shortcuts automation for Apple Watch workouts.

Does TrackOut change my Health data?
No. TrackOut only reads from Health and never writes to it.
